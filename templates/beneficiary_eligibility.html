FOODLOOP PARTICIPATION RULES

What your organisation agrees to

  • Use each collection only for the intended purpose shown on its listing. Your organisation may run feeding, livestock and composting projects.
  • Do not sell, exchange or barter food received through FoodLoop, or pass it to another organisation without FoodLoop’s written approval.
  • Request only what your organisation can collect, handle and store safely. Inspect food at collection; FoodLoop does not certify food as safe.
  • Use collected materials only for the purpose shown on each listing and keep basic collection and usage records. Human-food programmes must distribute fairly and without discrimination.
  • Give accurate information and cooperate with reasonable application checks and follow-up verification.

Approved beneficiaries can browse and request from every recovery pathway, including human food, animal feed and composting. Your organisation must be able to handle and use each collection for its listed purpose. Approval and food availability are not guaranteed. Applications are reviewed against community need, safe handling capacity, programme fit and available surplus. There is no minimum beneficiary count or minimum operating history.

WHAT TO PREPARE

Evidence depends on your organisation

Be ready to describe your programme, the people or animals it serves, or your composting operation, your leadership or sponsoring body, and how food will be collected, stored and distributed. You may provide the registration or recognition document that applies to your organisation. Relevant documents can include an NGO or society certificate, school or institution authorization, or a health-facility licence. Community programmes without a formal certificate can explain their structure and provide a suitable local reference.

If your premises prepare, store or handle food, provide any local-authority food-safety clearance that applies to those activities. If a requirement does not apply or is still pending, explain that in the application; do not upload unrelated documents.

Document requirements depend on your organisation’s type and activities. If you are unsure what applies, explain your situation in the application and FoodLoop will guide you during review.

Zambian laws and official guidance
  • MCDSS NGO standards and registration guidance — NGO registration, governance and records.
  • Ministry of Home Affairs: Registrar of Societies — society registration information.
  • Food Safety Act No. 7 of 2019 — food-premises compliance is handled by the relevant local authority.
  • Children’s Code Act No. 12 of 2022 — child care, protection and safeguarding.
  • Persons with Disabilities Act No. 6 of 2012 — registration and regulation for relevant disability institutions and organisations.
  • Ministry of Education: school feeding programme — current programme context for school applicants.
