TERMS OF USE

Terms of use

FoodLoop Zambia connects surplus-food donors with verified recipients and recovery partners. It coordinates recovery and collection; it does not inspect or certify food as safe.

For donors

Describe food honestly, including its condition, quantity, storage and collection deadline. Choose the highest suitable pathway: human consumption, animal feed, then composting. Food marked unsuitable for people must never be listed for human consumption. You remain responsible for the accuracy of your listing.

For recipients and recovery partners

Inspect the food at collection, use it only for the purpose shown on its listing, and do not resell recovered food. Confirm receipt only after the handover. Report misdescribed or unsafe listings through the report form.

Donations and collections

Listings are donations by default. FoodLoop does not process payments or guarantee financial transactions. Any exchange of money is outside the platform. Both parties confirm collections, and only confirmed quantities contribute to recovery figures. Partial collection is not supported in FoodLoop.

Access and moderation

Donors can publish immediately after registration. Beneficiaries need account approval before requesting collections, and may request from any recovery pathway when able to handle and use the item appropriately. Administrators may reject or suspend access and remove listings, recording the reason in the audit trail. Exact pickup addresses and contact details are shared only with accepted counterparts and authorised administrators.

Donors and recipients remain responsible for accurate descriptions, appropriate handling and inspection at collection.
