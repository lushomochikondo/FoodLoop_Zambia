OUR PROGRAMMES

Recover food through the right pathway.

Each listing is matched to a suitable next use, based on the food’s condition and the receiving organisation’s ability to handle it.

01 / HUMAN CONSUMPTION

Food for people

Safe, suitable surplus can reach verified organisations serving local communities.

02 / LIVESTOCK FEED

Food for feed

Appropriate surplus can be collected by approved livestock and feed partners.

03 / ORGANIC RECOVERY

Composting and organics

Material unsuitable for people or feed can be directed to composting and organic-waste recovery.

HOW FOODLOOP WORKS

A clear route from surplus to collection.

Food businesses list what is available. Verified organisations request suitable items. Donors and recipients confirm the handover.
