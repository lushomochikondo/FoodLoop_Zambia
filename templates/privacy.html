PRIVACY NOTICE

How FoodLoop uses your data.

We collect account names and email addresses for sign-in and communication; organisation contacts, locations and verification evidence for eligibility review; and listings, requests and collection records to coordinate recovery and report impact. If you use the contact form, we store your name, email address, subject and message so FoodLoop administrators can respond.

Who can see it

Your account profile is visible to you and administrators. Organisation names and general areas appear to eligible counterparties. Exact pickup addresses and contact phone numbers are revealed after acceptance. Verification references and evidence, plus contact form messages, are available to administrators. Activity records are limited to the relevant parties and administrators.

Food photos

Photos are visible to users authorised to view the listing. Uploaded files are verified, resized and re-encoded, removing embedded EXIF metadata. Avoid including faces, private documents or contact information in photos.

Security and sessions

Passwords are hashed. Access checks run on the server, and hosted deployment uses HTTPS and secure session cookies. The app uses a session cookie and CSRF protection; the installable app does not cache private pages or uploaded food photos for offline use.

Retention and questions

Operational records support collection coordination, accountability and reporting. Contact FoodLoop through the website form with questions about account data, record retention or corrections.
