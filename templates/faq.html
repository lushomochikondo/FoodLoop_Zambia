Frequently asked questions

Registration, listings and collection procedures.

How do I create an account?

Select Create an account, choose your organisation type, and provide the requested information. An administrator reviews the organisation before it can publish or request food.

Who approves a collection request?

The food donor accepts or declines requests for its own listings. Administrators verify organisations and review reported concerns.

When is the quantity reserved?

Stock is reserved when the donor accepts a request. A pending request does not reserve stock. Accepted quantities cannot exceed what remains available.

When can I see the pickup address?

Before acceptance, a listing displays its general area. Exact pickup addresses and contact details become available to the accepted recipient after acceptance.

How is collection confirmed?

The donor marks the request ready, confirms handover of the accepted quantity, and the recipient confirms receipt. Only then does the quantity count as collected.

Can I delete a listing?

Donors can delete their own listings from the listing details page. Active requests are cancelled and reservations released. Existing collection and moderation history is retained where required for accountability.

What happens if a collection is missed?

Requests that remain uncollected after the deadline become No-show and release the reserved quantity. Expired listings cannot receive new requests.

How do I enable email notifications?

Add your email address under Account settings and enable email updates. Notifications are also available in the app.

Does FoodLoop certify food safety?

FoodLoop coordinates food recovery. Donors are responsible for accurate descriptions and recipients must inspect food on collection and use it only for their approved pathway.
