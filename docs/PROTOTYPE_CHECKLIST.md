# Five-minute prototype walkthrough

1. Sign in as admin. Review the fictional pending organisation and record an approval reason. Show the new audit entry.
2. Sign in as provider. Create a human-consumption donation with a food photo, positive quantity and future deadline. Read the safety acknowledgement.
3. Sign in as recipient in a second browser profile. Show matching reasons and the hidden exact address. Submit a quantity request.
4. As provider, show the new notification and accept the request. As recipient, show that the pickup address is now visible.
5. Show that a second acceptance cannot exceed the remaining quantity.
6. As provider, mark Ready for Collection and confirm the full handover. Show that one confirmation alone does not add impact.
7. As recipient, confirm receipt. Open Your impact: listed versus collected, pathway totals, estimated meals, and CSV export.
8. Report a sample listing as recipient. As admin, review and dismiss the report or remove the listing with a reason. Show the audit entry.
9. Briefly show the mobile menu and photo-upload form at phone width.

Before the event: run migrations and tests, create a fresh backup, check all six account passwords, ensure the chosen listings have future deadlines, rehearse in separate browser profiles, and test the local copy with networking disconnected. Prepare a backup recording. Public hosting, venue/location confirmation and the final acknowledgement wording remain operational decisions.
